Selling AI tools isn’t about pitching tech. It’s about cutting through noise, hitting pain points fast, and getting to the damn decision. When I partnered with [Predis AI](https://predis.ai?ref=ryan89) as an affiliate, I knew one thing: if I could get someone on the phone, I could close them.

In under three months, I generated $3,500/month in recurring commissions — all through cold calling. No paid ads. No inbound. Just pure outbound pressure, delivered with precision and psychology. This is how I did it.

### Picking Up the Phone When No One Else Would

Every day, I built my own cold call lists: agency owners, freelancers, boutique marketers, and e-commerce operators — the ones drowning in content creation. These weren’t warm leads. They didn’t know me, and they’d never heard of Predis.

But once I had them on the line, I didn’t talk features. I talked about the three hours they wasted that morning designing Instagram posts. I talked about the VA they were paying to copy-paste ChatGPT output. I hit the frustration and showed them the exit — Predis.AI. Full content workflows. One tool. Immediate relief.

### Pitching Value with Ruthless Efficiency

Every call had a framework:

  - **Agitate** the problem

  - **Isolate** the cost

  - **Position** Predis as the smarter path

  - **Close** fast, before objections compound

No fluff, no slides, no “checking in.” I used psychology — future pacing, value anchoring, loss aversion — to make the “yes” inevitable. I offered them free onboarding if they signed up same-day. If they hesitated, I stacked bonuses. If they resisted, I dismantled objections until there was nowhere to run but forward.

### High Retention Starts at the Close

Because these deals were closed over real conversations — not casual clicks — retention was high. I wasn’t selling to browsers; I was selling to business owners who needed a system and trusted my word. I followed up with onboarding help, setup walkthroughs, and personalized follow-up. That built trust. That kept churn low.

### The Results

  - **$3,500+ MRR** in under 90 days

  - **100% cold calling** — no ads, no SEO, no warm leads

  - **High retention** due to personalized sales and support

The strategy was aggressive, intentional, and built for volume. I didn’t wait for deals — I created them. Every call had one goal: close fast, close clean, and make it stick.
